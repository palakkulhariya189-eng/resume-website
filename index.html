Venture Concept

Drift Buddy

✕

The Problem

Many students make academic and career decisions based on a narrow set of familiar professions, without practical exposure to what different careers actually entail.

The Concept

Drift Buddy was conceptualized as a student-focused career exposure platform that helps learners explore diverse career possibilities, understand real-world roles, and discover paths aligned with their curiosity before committing blindly.

Key Takeaway

Defining the user problem clearly before jumping into features ensures the solution addresses genuine student confusion rather than surface-level information overload.

Field Research

Car Detailing Business

✕

The Problem

Customers handing over their vehicles for detailing often experience uncertainty around what work is actually being completed, whether timelines will hold, and how quality is verified.

The Concept

Through business research and workflow mapping, the project explored practical ways to close the transparency and accountability gap—such as milestone-based service updates, clear delivery commitments, and visible documentation of work performed.

Key Takeaway

In high-trust service businesses, customer satisfaction depends just as much on communication and visibility during the process as on the final physical outcome.

B2B Exposure

ReadyAssist

✕

The Context

Exposure to ReadyAssist provided an inside look at how a 24/7 roadside assistance company manages urgent, location-sensitive vehicle breakdowns across cities.

What I Learned

The company visit highlighted how ReadyAssist operates through structured B2B partnerships and an organized network of trained service technicians—balancing speed, partner coordination, and consistent service standards.

Key Takeaway

Seeing a live operational floor demonstrated how B2B distribution channels and partner networks allow a service business to scale without owning every physical asset.
